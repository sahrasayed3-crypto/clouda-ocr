# Source Attribution Guidance

This metadata release preserves the exact upstream dataset identifiers used by
the benchmark. Any downstream use must review `source_summary.csv`, preserve
applicable notices, and cite the corresponding dataset and paper.

## KITAB-Bench-derived records

The 30 records retain these released dataset identifiers:

- `ahmedheakl/arocrbench_doclaynet`
- `ahmedheakl/arocrbench_hindawi`
- `ahmedheakl/arocrbench_historicalbooks`
- `ahmedheakl/arocrbench_historyar`
- `ahmedheakl/arocrbench_khattparagraph`

Preserve the released-asset notice and cite KITAB-Bench. Dataset identifiers
remain visible because some released subsets originate from external sources.

## CALFA records

The 30 CALFA records use `calfa-ai/tarima`, `calfa-ai/RASAM-1`,
`calfa-ai/RASAM-2`, `calfa-ai/baybars`, and `calfa-ai/iskandar`. Cite the
relevant CALFA, TariMa, RASAM, Baybars, or Iskandar dataset and papers. Original
library-image and distorted-derivative redistribution remains unresolved and
is not authorized by this metadata release.

## Other source families

- Cite `mohres/The_Arabic_E-Book_Corpus` for its 20 records and preserve its
  applicable attribution notice.
- Cite `MohamedRashad/arabic-img2md` and the associated Arabic Nougat work for
  its 15 records.
- Cite `craneset/arabic-ocr` and preserve the free-sample notice for its five
  records. Original sample assets must not be redistributed.

This file supplies attribution guidance, not a grant of rights. The separate
permission fields in the public manifests control the integration posture.
